# App Brainstorming — Group Milestone 1

## Initial App Ideas (6+)

1. **FlowFit** — Fitness + nutrition tracker that combines workout logging, food tracking, and goal‑based suggestions in one app.
2. **StudyFlow** — Study planner that breaks big assignments into daily tasks, tracks progress, and sends reminders.
3. **MealMate** — Simple weekly meal planner with grocery list generation and budget awareness.
4. **MindWave** — Mood and habit tracker that helps users correlate habits (sleep, exercise, screen time) with mood trends.
5. **EventEase** — Lightweight event organizer for small groups (family, friends, clubs) with shared checklists and reminders.
6. **SkillStack** — Personal skill‑building tracker where users set learning goals (coding, language, music) and log practice sessions.
7. **AquaGuard** — Water intake + hydration reminder app with simple charts and streaks.

---

## Top 3 Ideas — Evaluation
1. FlowFit – Fitness + Nutrition Tracker
Mobile: Perfect for quick logging, daily streaks, and on‑the‑go workout tracking.
Story: Helps users stay consistent with fitness and nutrition.
Market: Students, beginners, and casual fitness users.
Habit: Daily meal/workout logging encourages repeat use.
Scope: Very feasible for a 4‑week sprint; features can be built incrementally.

2. StudyBuddy – Student Planner
Mobile: Students check deadlines and reminders constantly on their phones.
Story: Solves the problem of missed assignments and poor organization.
Market: High school and college students.
Habit: Daily task check‑ins and reminders build habit loops.
Scope: Medium; calendar + reminders add complexity but still doable.

3. MoodMate – Mood + Habit Tracker
Mobile: Quick daily mood check‑ins are ideal for mobile.
Story: Helps users understand emotional patterns and build healthy habits.
Market: Anyone interested in mental wellness.
Habit: Daily mood logging + streaks encourage consistent use.
Scope: Medium; charts require extra work but still manageable.

Final Choice
We selected FlowFit because it has the best balance of feasibility, clarity, and sprint‑friendly features.


### 1. FlowFit

**Description:**  
A unified fitness app that combines workout tracking, nutrition logging, and goal‑based suggestions (cutting, bulking, or maintenance).

**Pros:**
- Strong real‑world demand (fitness + nutrition is a huge space).
- Clear two‑pronged value: food + workouts in one place.
- Easy to scope an MVP (basic logging + suggestions).
- Visually appealing UI possibilities (charts, stats, progress).
- Great for a group: can split into nutrition, workouts, backend, and UI.

**Cons:**
- Full “dream” version could be very large if not scoped carefully.
- Requires discipline to keep features focused for MVP.

---

### 2. StudyFlow

**Description:**  
A study planner that breaks assignments into smaller tasks, schedules them across days, and tracks completion.

**Pros:**
- Very relatable for students.
- Simple data models (tasks, deadlines, courses).
- Easy to demo and test.
- Clear user stories and flows.

**Cons:**
- Less visually exciting than a fitness app.
- Many similar apps already exist; harder to stand out.

---

### 3. MindWave

**Description:**  
A mood and habit tracker that lets users log mood, habits, and see correlations over time.

**Pros:**
- Mental health and self‑awareness are strong themes.
- Interesting data visualization opportunities.
- Flexible feature set (mood logs, tags, habits).

**Cons:**
- Requires thoughtful UX to avoid feeling generic.
- Correlation/insight features could get complex if over‑scoped.

---

## Final App Idea Chosen

### ✅ **Chosen App: FlowFit**

We chose **FlowFit** because:

- It solves a real, common problem: people often use separate apps for workouts and nutrition.
- The two‑pronged approach (nutrition + exercise) gives it a clear identity and competitive edge.
- It supports a wide audience, from beginners to experienced lifters.
- It naturally divides work across four team members (nutrition, workouts, backend, UI/UX).
- It is ambitious but still realistic to implement as an MVP within the project timeline.

FlowFit will focus on:

- Logging workouts and meals.
- Providing simple, goal‑based suggestions (e.g., cutting vs bulking).
- Showing basic progress and stats in a clean, intuitive interface.
